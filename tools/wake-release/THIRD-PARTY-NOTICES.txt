Fono Lira Beta inference package

Fono code and the small Lira candidate model: MIT, see worker/LICENSE-Fono.
The candidate is unchanged from the synthetic/public-data development model.
No owner voice, personal recordings or training corpus is distributed.
Training provenance: synthetic utterances generated without reference audio,
plus 20 negative ordinary-speech clips from google/fleurs by the FLEURS authors
(Google), revision 437d9e667fa3a2a08a1957f810bf2c3b5050d221, CC-BY-4.0:
https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The training pipeline resampled/cropped those clips; no dataset audio is bundled.
